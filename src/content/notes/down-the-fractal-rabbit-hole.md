An interconnected worldview can make fifteen websites feel like one explanation. The difficulty is deciding whether the connections are supported by evidence or simply repeated across fifteen domains.

The starting point was a question:

> “What are the weirdest sites or apps you've visited or used, anon?”

The example supplied was [fauux.neocities.org](https://fauux.neocities.org/), a website titled *Wired Sound for Wired People*. Its pages draw on *Serial Experiments Lain*, music, imagery and experimental web culture. In a retrospective post, its creator explains that its distinctive dithering emerged from a practical constraint: Neocities' original 10 MB storage limit forced images to be compressed. A technical restriction became part of its visual identity.

Alongside that recommendation came another statement:

> “one guy made all of these”

What followed was a list of fifteen domains, covering everything from fractal geometry and consciousness to water treatment, clothing and economics.

The anonymous attribution is unverified. Fauux is a separate creative project; no affiliation with the fifteen domains is established here.

The single-creator claim needs qualification too. The listed sites revolve around Dan Winter's work, but several explicitly credit collaborators. [FlameInMind](https://flameinmind.com/), for example, names both Winter and Patrick Botte. This is a network organised around a central body of ideas, with multiple contributors.

## FIFTEEN DOORS INTO A SHARED WORLDVIEW

[FractalField](https://fractalfield.com/), [GoldenMean](https://www.goldenmean.info/) and [ImplosionGroup](https://implosiongroup.com/) provide the broadest entrances. Their pages bring together lectures, books, diagrams, project announcements and links to related ventures.

The recurring vocabulary is distinctive: fractals, the golden ratio, coherence, implosion, phase conjugation and negentropy. These concepts connect subjects usually discussed separately: physics, consciousness, spiritual experience, biological processes and technological design.

Beneath the specialised language lies a relatively simple proposition: the relationships that organise nature might also offer clues to how people think, feel, create and live together.

“Fractal coherence” is a useful description of that overarching theme. It captures the interest in recurring patterns and harmonious organisation without assuming that every proposed connection has been demonstrated.

## A PATTERN IS NOT YET A MECHANISM

A fractal involves patterns that recur across scales. The golden ratio is a mathematical proportion. Winter's framework assigns these concepts a much broader explanatory role.

In [*Planck-Phire*](https://goldenmean.info/planckphire/), he proposes that particular fractal and harmonic relationships allow electrical charge to concentrate through a process described as perfected charge collapse or implosion. He links this proposed mechanism to gravity, consciousness and the emergence of order.

“Negentropy” is another organising term. Throughout the network, it is associated with the formation or maintenance of order, and with Winter's ideas about life and awareness.

The existence of fractals and mathematical proportions does not, by itself, establish the proposed mechanisms connecting them to consciousness or gravity. The distinction matters because the websites move between mathematical description, physical explanation and spiritual interpretation.

Reading them carefully means asking which kind of statement is being made at each stage. Recognising a pattern is the beginning of an investigation. It does not complete it.

## WHEN LEARNING BECOMES EXPERIENCE

[FractalU](https://fractalu.com/) gives the ideas an educational home through lectures, courses and discussions. Its programme connects sacred geometry with consciousness and related themes, providing a route through a substantial collection of material.

[JediSchool](https://www.jedischool.science/) takes a more imaginative approach, framing consciousness education through heightened awareness and human potential. Its pages include claims about unusual perceptual abilities, including “seeing without eyes”. Those claims require controlled evidence; a demonstration or testimonial alone cannot settle them.

Across both projects, the intention is to make learning experiential. Concepts about pattern and resonance become things participants might investigate through attention, perception and personal practice.

That creates a further distinction. An experience can be meaningful to the person having it without establishing the explanation offered for it.

## MEASUREMENT AND INTERPRETATION

[FlameInMind](https://flameinmind.com/), [iTHRVE](https://ithrve.com/) and [HRV-App](https://www.hrv-app.com/) bring the discussion towards bodily signals.

FlameInMind presents brainwave biofeedback alongside meditation and sound-based tools. iTHRVE centres on heart-rate variability, breathing and related feedback functions, while HRV-App supplies explanations and instructions within the same project network.

Their shared ambition is to connect subjective experience with observable information: to give users feedback while they practise breathing, relaxation or focused attention.

Recording a physiological signal is different from demonstrating that it measures empathy, spiritual development or “life force”. Each wider interpretation requires evidence beyond the existence of the signal itself.

Within the collection's broader story, these apps serve as a bridge between abstract ideas and activities a person can undertake directly. The bridge is useful only if we remain clear about what is being measured and what is being inferred.

## THE THEORY BECOMES A DEVICE

The technological projects extend the same ideas into the surrounding environment.

TheImploder applies the group's implosion framework to water. Associated [project pages](https://www.fractalfield.com/implodernews/) describe vortex and magnetic arrangements and report claimed effects on water quality and plant growth. These materials document what the project proposes and reports; they do not remove the need to examine testing methods and independent replication.

[TheraPhi](https://www.theraphi.net/) and [PhiRay](https://www.phiray.com/) form the plasma-related branch. The current TheraPhi page directs visitors towards PhiRay, which is presented as a newer system combining plasma and magnetic components. PhiRay's own website describes the system as experimental and states that it makes no medical claims. That qualification is essential context for its testimonials and proposed benefits.

[PyramidWirelessPower](https://www.pyramidwirelesspower.com/) gives the framework an architectural and historical dimension. It proposes that pyramid geometry can interact with Earth resonances to support wireless energy transmission. The website presents this as part of Winter's wider explanation of coherent fields and implosion; a proposed mechanism is different from a demonstrated, operational energy system.

Together, these projects express an ambition to translate a theory of natural organisation into practical devices. Each translation creates a separate obligation to show that the device does what is claimed.

## FROM CLOTHING TO ECONOMICS

The framework's reach becomes particularly apparent in FractalClothing and FractalEconomics.

Related [Implosion Group material](https://implosiongroup.com/) connects the clothing project with ideas about materials, capacitance and the body's environment. The standalone FractalClothing website could not be retrieved during this review, so its content should not be described more confidently than that supporting material allows.

[FractalEconomics](https://www.fractaleconomics.net/) extends the argument into resource distribution. It draws an analogy between the organisation of a fractal field and the organisation of a society, proposing that effective sharing should connect individual wellbeing with the wellbeing of the whole.

This is a substantial conceptual step. A geometric relationship does not automatically yield a workable economic model. Yet the extension reveals the collection's aspiration: patterns of organisation might inform both personal experience and collective life.

## ONE FRAMEWORK, MANY APPLICATIONS

Across the collection, the recurring ambition is to connect natural patterns with consciousness, technology and everyday life. The projects range from educational archives and biofeedback tools to experimental devices and proposals for social organisation.

Their shared vocabulary gives the network continuity. Each application nevertheless stands on its own evidence: a recorded physiological signal, a proposed physical mechanism and an economic analogy are different kinds of claim. Their relationship is the subject of the enquiry, rather than proof of its conclusions.
